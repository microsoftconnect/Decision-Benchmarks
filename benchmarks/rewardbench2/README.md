## What we changed

Microsoft adapted this benchmark to the Microsoft-Decision-1 request format (a `state` and typed `questions`, with the source's answers as `labels`). For each prompt, showed four of the source's completions (the preferred ones and rejected ones) in shuffled order and asked which is best. The answer is the source's preferred completion.

## Notes

- **Content note:** the Safety subset (450 items) contains requests a model should refuse or answer with care (including sexually explicit, hateful or derogatory, misinformation, privacy-invading and impossible requests), and some of the completions to choose from comply with them. They are there to test whether a model prefers the safe response.
- The completions were written by third-party language models (listed on the RewardBench 2 dataset card) and remain subject to those models' terms.
